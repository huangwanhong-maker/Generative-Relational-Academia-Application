# Contributing

Use this repository for academia-specific workflows, the native GRRP implementation and its web application. Explain how a change affects research acts, provenance, contestation and portability. Preserve source history and distinguish a proposed shared-protocol adapter from a completed semantic migration. Keep private research records, accounts and keys outside the source repository.

The project-authored material is offered under the [MIT licence](LICENSE), within the [current scope](LICENSE.md). By intentionally submitting original work for incorporation, you offer it under MIT while retaining your copyright. Identify third-party material, its terms and your authority to contribute it; access to a research record alone is not permission to publish it.

Add your preferred contributor name or organization and a short contribution description to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) when your work is incorporated. Changes to GRRP also update its packaged contributor list. Preserve prior author and organizational credits, external notices and historical licensing artifacts. AI tools may be acknowledged as tools, not as copyright holders.
